🔗 API ENDPOINTS

       Submit: {name, email, phone, national_id, service}
       Returns: {success, message, id}

       Returns: {price, subscription, volume, min_shares, min_investment}

       Submit: {email, shares}
       Returns: {success, message, amount, subscription_id}

       Returns: {registrations, active}

       Returns: {status, timestamp}
